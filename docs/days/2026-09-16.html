Why Apple, Google and other global tech giants will hate the ATO’s latest ruling on cross-border payments

The Australian Taxation Office (ATO) has issued a new ruling that treats offshore software payments made by multinational tech giants, including Apple, Google, Microsoft, and Amazon, as taxable royalties. This regulatory shift is designed to prevent these companies from shifting profits earned from Australian consumers to low-tax offshore jurisdictions. The decision could significantly increase the tax liabilities of these tech giants in Australia and potentially spark a tax dispute with the United States.

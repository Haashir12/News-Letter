The tech industry is hot for shopping bots, but they're years away

The tech and financial industries are increasingly focused on "agentic commerce," where AI agents make online purchases on behalf of consumers, though practical implementation remains years away. Major e-commerce platforms like Amazon have resisted the trend, recently blocking Meta's Muse bot and Perplexity's AI bot from accessing their services. Conversely, payment processors like Mastercard are encouraging the development, releasing a report on the infrastructure needed to support automated AI transactions.

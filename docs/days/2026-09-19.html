India forces caller-ID apps to feed spam reports to telcos

The Telecom Regulatory Authority of India (TRAI) has updated its anti-spam rules to mandate that caller-ID and call-management apps share user-reported spam data with telecom operators. This data must be sent to a blockchain-based platform maintained by telcos to track commercial communications and enforce anti-spam regulations. Truecaller has criticized the decision as anti-competitive, arguing it forces the company to hand over a commercially valuable proprietary asset to telecom operators.

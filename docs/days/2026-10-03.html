Sanders introduces bill to ban the federal government from using Flock

US Senator Bernie Sanders has introduced the Ban Flock Act, which aims to prohibit federal agencies from using automatic license plate readers (ALPRs). The proposed legislation would also prevent federal agencies from accessing ALPR data collected by local police departments and private companies like Flock Safety. While the bill's title references Flock, the legislative text applies broadly to all ALPR systems, with only limited exceptions.
